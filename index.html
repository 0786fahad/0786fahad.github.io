About Ledger Tools

Ledger Tools is a small collection of free, no-sign-up calculators for everyday math: crypto profit and loss, loan EMI, percentage problems, unit conversion, age, and stairs/tile area and cost. Every calculation runs directly in your browser — nothing you type is sent to a server or stored anywhere. The goal is simple: quick, accurate answers without ads getting in the way of the numbers you actually need.

These tools are built for students, traders, and anyone who wants a fast answer without installing an app or creating an account. New calculators get added over time based on what people find useful.
